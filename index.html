About Me

I grew up in Grand Rapids, Michigan and now study Finance and Management at Xavier University's Williams College of Business. I love the numbers side of business, but what actually gets me out of bed is the people side — building trust with a team, listening to someone who needs it, or figuring out how to make an organization run a little better for everyone in it.

Outside the classroom, I've found that same energy in some unexpected places: co-facilitating wellness workshops as a peer educator, researching donors for a nonprofit supporting people in addiction recovery, and building relationships one honest conversation at a time. I'm still figuring a lot out, and I like it that way — staying curious is kind of the whole point.
